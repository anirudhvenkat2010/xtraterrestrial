      Quick Glossary of NASA Mission Terms
      PLSS (Portable Life Support): Astronaut backpack providing O₂, CO₂ scrubbing, and battery power (480-min limit, 60-min reserve).
      MOLA & HiRISE: Orbital laser altimeter and 25cm/px camera used to map steep >20° cliffs and boulder hazards.
      CRISM Spectra: Hyperspectral mineral mapper pinpointing ancient water-altered carbonates and smectite clays.
      DONKI SEP: NASA space weather alert system tracking Solar Energetic Particle radiation storms.

      NASA MULTI-MISSION DATA PROVENANCE & API ARCHITECTURE

      AresNav integrates live NASA web endpoints with synthetic scenario terrain (not downloaded PDS rasters) over Jezero Crater (18.4447°N, 77.4508°E) to power real-time A* physics routing and closed-loop life-support modeling:

      1. NASA Solar System Treks (Mars Trek WMTS)
      Streams MGS MOLA Colorized Hillshade (Mars_MGS_MOLA_ClrShade_merge_global_463m) and Viking MDIM 2.1 orbital mosaic tiles directly into the Leaflet canvas via OGC WMTS REST endpoints, backed by a local HTML5 Canvas topographic contour generator.
      2. NASA JPL MMGIS / Mars 2020 Perseverance Traverse
      Overlays Perseverance's actual driven coordinates from Octavia E. Butler Landing (Sol 0) through Three Forks Depot (Sol 705), Margin Unit (Sol 1000), and Bright Angel (Sol 1150) to benchmark human EVA routes.
      3. NASA Mars 2020 Rover Photos API (api.nasa.gov/mars-photos)
      Queries real Perseverance Mastcam-Z and Navcam surface images by Sol so astronauts can verify outcrop morphology and regolith traction before committing PLSS oxygen.
      4. NASA CCMC DONKI Space Weather API (api.nasa.gov/DONKI)
      Fetches Solar Energetic Particle (SEP) and Solar Flare alerts to trigger radiation-shelter walk-back abort routing.
